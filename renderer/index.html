    Settings

    ClearMail couldn't unlock your saved app password or Claude key from the macOS Keychain. Quit and reopen ClearMail and choose Always Allow when asked, or type them again below. Leaving the fields blank keeps the saved ones.

    Email account

    SSL/TLS usually uses ports 993 and 465. STARTTLS usually uses 143 and 587.

    Use an app password, not your normal password. Gmail: myaccount.google.com/apppasswords · iCloud: account.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords. Changing the provider, address or server clears the saved password, so type it again.

    AI summaries (optional)

    Without a key, ClearMail still removes waffle offline. With a key, every email gets a plain-English one-liner, a to-do list and suggested replies.
